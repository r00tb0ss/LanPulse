# LanPulse 1.25.45

## For troubleshooting: see which speed fields UniFi gives for a device
A new read-only page, `/api/unifi/rates?q=<device name, IP or MAC>`, shows the speed and traffic numbers UniFi returns for a device. It reads them from UniFi's client list, from the device's switch port and from the newer client list the UniFi app uses, and shows them next to what LanPulse displays. It returns numbers only. It is meant for working out why a device's speed looks different in LanPulse and in the UniFi app, for example virtual machines that share a switch port.
