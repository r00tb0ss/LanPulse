# LanPulse 1.25.40

## Download and upload for wired devices
In the topology's **Traffic** mode, wired devices now show separate download and upload, with the blue and purple flow along their cables, the same as Wi-Fi devices and the switch and access point links. Their numbers are read at the switch port, where which way is which is clear. The **Swap ↑↓** button only affects Wi-Fi devices and the switch, access point and internet links; wired devices don't need it.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates > Download > Install. Your devices, history, settings and login are kept.
