# LanPulse 1.25.35

## Two new themes
**Frost** is a dark theme in cool slate with ice-blue accents and soft contrast. **Sand** is a warm paper light theme with terracotta accents. Pick them in Settings > Appearance.

## Light themes: readable map text
In Enterprise Light and Sand the "Live attack map" title and the map legend were dark text on the dark map, and the red "Reached your LAN" tile was hard to read. Both are fixed.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
