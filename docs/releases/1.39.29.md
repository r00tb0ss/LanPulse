# LanPulse 1.39.29

## Public scan tab inside Threats
- Threats now has two sections at the top: **Attacks** (the map and attacker details) and **Public scan**.
- The authorized public-IP scan (Nmap or OpenVAS) moved from Vulns to the new Public scan section, with its own scan history.
- You can delete a finished scan from the history with its trash button, or use **Clear finished** to remove them all. Queued and running scans are never deleted.
- Deleting history does not reset the scan limits: the 60-second global and 1-hour per-target waits still apply to scans you deleted.
