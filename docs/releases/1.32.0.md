# 1.32.0

## Quiet mode
- New bell button in the top bar. Press it to mute alert messages for 1 hour, 4 hours or until 8 AM, for example while you work on the network or run a scan. Press it again to turn alerts back on early.
- While muted the bell turns amber and shows when alerts come back, plus how many were held back.
- LanPulse keeps watching and recording while muted. Everything still shows in the dashboard; only the messages to ntfy, Telegram, email and the other channels are held back. The daily summary still goes out, and the Save & send test button still works.
- Quiet mode ends by itself, so it can't be left on by accident.
