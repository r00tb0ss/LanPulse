# 1.39.2

The IP info box opens quickly again, and its size is back to normal.

- **Faster.** The outside lookups (reverse DNS, ISP, Shodan, AbuseIPDB, GreyNoise, ThreatFox, Tor list) used to run one after another, so a single slow or unreachable service could keep the box on "Looking up…" for 15 seconds or more. They now run side by side with a 9 second limit. The box opens right away with what LanPulse already knows (attempts, targets, the behaviour read), and the ISP and abuse details fill in as they arrive. A lookup that took too long is retried after two minutes instead of being remembered for six hours.
- **Normal size, with a wider option.** On a tablet the side panel was covering the whole screen. It now keeps its normal width, and a new button next to the close button makes it wider or returns it to normal. The choice is remembered.
- The browser check in CI now tests the panel size at tablet width.

No change to any detection, score or data.
