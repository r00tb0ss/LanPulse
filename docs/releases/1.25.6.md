# LanPulse 1.25.6

## Topology
- New **Spread** view (the default): every device is its own node with its own line to the switch or access point it connects to, instead of sitting in a box. Turn Spread off to go back to grouped boxes.
- Drag any device to move it. Positions are remembered in the browser; **Reset** puts them back.
- Traffic: each device's line animates toward its switch or access point, gets thicker with more connections, and shows its count per hour under the device. The scale is logarithmic, so quiet devices still show next to a very busy one.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
