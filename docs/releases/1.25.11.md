# LanPulse 1.25.11

## Threats
- The IP details panel now includes honeypot activity: attempts, first and last seen, the services it hit, the logins it tried and what it sent. Before, an address that only ever hit the honeypot showed 0 attempts and an empty "What it tried".
- Honeypot hits no longer count as "reached your LAN" there.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
