# LanPulse 1.25.15

## Operations Center
A new row of live tiles along the bottom of the wall-display page:
- **Network health:** healthy, fair or down at a glance, how many switches, access points and the gateway are up (and which are offline), and how many Wi-Fi clients have a weak signal.
- **Services:** how many monitored services are up, which are down, and the slowest one.
- **Honeypot:** hits in the last 24 hours, anything from inside your own network, the top attacker and the most-tried login.
- **Busiest devices:** the devices making the most connections in the last hour.

TV polish:
- An "updated" time in the header, and a red banner if LanPulse stops answering, so a stale screen can't pass as live.
- A dim button (moon icon) for a TV in a dark room; it remembers your choice.
- The map and Top sources now also appear if the Operations Center is the first page you open.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
