# LanPulse 1.25.24

## OpenVAS scan buttons outside Settings
- **Device panel:** a new "OpenVAS scan" section shows when that device was last scanned (or the scan's progress while it runs) with a **Scan now** button.
- **Vulnerabilities tab:** each device in the device list has an **OpenVAS scan** button.

The buttons only appear once OpenVAS is installed. If it's installed but switched off or not ready yet, the panel says so and points to Settings > Scanner. Scan all and the weekly automatic scan are still in Settings.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
