# LanPulse 1.25.41

## A cleaner topology
The topology now uses one blue for every cable, dashed blue for Wi-Fi links and neutral icons, like the UniFi topology. The other schemes are still under **Color by** (device type, status, security risk, zone, Wi-Fi signal or new devices) whenever you want one. The new look is the default, so a Color by choice you saved earlier is reset once.

## Offline devices can be hidden
Click **Online**, or untick **Offline**, and offline devices now disappear from the topology instead of only fading. Selecting **Offline** shows only the offline ones. Switches, access points and the gateway always stay on the map, and a search or the other filters still dim instead of hiding.

## Traffic updates by itself
With **Traffic** on, the topology refreshes its speeds about every 5 seconds while you are looking at it, so you no longer need the refresh button. When nothing is watching, LanPulse goes back to checking UniFi every 30 seconds. UniFi measures speeds over a short window itself, so the numbers run a few seconds behind, as in the UniFi app.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates > Download > Install. Your devices, history, settings and login are kept.
