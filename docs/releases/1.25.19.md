# LanPulse 1.25.19

## Topology: traffic on every switch and access point link
Switches and access points now report the speed of their own uplink, so every link in the Traffic view shows flow, including switches with no clients listed behind them and traffic from devices UniFi doesn't list individually. Links above are at least as busy as the busiest link below.

Wired devices that UniFi gives no speed for now use the speed of the switch port they're plugged into.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
