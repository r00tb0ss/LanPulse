# LanPulse 1.36.6

## Honeypot YARA scan persistence
- Runs YARA when a captured sample is added and stores the scan status, ruleset fingerprint, and scan timestamp.
- Reuses a stored result when the active ruleset fingerprint is unchanged; rescans and refreshes the stored result when the ruleset changes.
- Exposes YARA scan status, match count, and scan time in captured-sample summaries.
- Includes YARA scan state in the AI analyst's sample context and clarifies that no YARA match does not prove a sample is safe.
- Improves the layout of expandable YARA result details.

## Safety
- Captured samples are analyzed as data only. LanPulse does not execute captured files.
