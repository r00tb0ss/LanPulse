# LanPulse 1.25.14

## Threat map
Fixes for the map being messy and laggy with Attacks and Device traffic both on.
- **Country labels no longer pile up.** Labels that would land on each other are skipped (busiest first), and zooming in brings the others back. With both layers on, labels show the name only and at most 8 appear.
- **Smoother panning and zooming.** The whole map used to be rebuilt about six times on every drag frame. It now settles once when you let go.
- **Calmer arcs.** Device-traffic arcs used to restart their travelling dots several times a second, so they stalled near your location. Redraws are now batched, and fewer traffic arcs show while attacks are on.
- Labels refresh after the zoom buttons and region picker finish moving the map.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
