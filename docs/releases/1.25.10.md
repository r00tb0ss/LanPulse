# LanPulse 1.25.10

## Honeypot
- New time range picker: 24 hours, 7, 30 or 90 days.
- Activity chart of hits over the chosen range.
- Every attacker and hit now shows country, city and who owns the network (for example DigitalOcean), plus first and last seen.
- Click any address, outside or internal, to see everything it did: every login it tried, every request it sent, which decoys it touched, with timestamps. A button there opens full IP intelligence and blocking.
- New cards: where attackers come from (by country), most guessed usernames, and most guessed passwords.
- Includes 1.25.9 (honeypot hits on the Threats map).

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
