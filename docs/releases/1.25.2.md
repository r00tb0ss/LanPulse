# LanPulse 1.25.2

## Topology view
- Zoom in and out with new + / - buttons, pinch on a touchscreen, double-tap/double-click, or the mouse wheel.
- The map now opens at a readable size. A very wide network opens centred on the gateway, so pan or pinch to explore it, instead of shrinking to tiny text.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
