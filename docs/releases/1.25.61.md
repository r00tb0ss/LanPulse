# LanPulse 1.25.61

## Changed
- **Threats map, Attacks layer:** with only **Attacks** selected, the map now keeps showing attack arcs even when the network is quiet. It replays your most recent attacks, one new arc every second and a half with up to three flying at once, and real new attacks still appear the moment they arrive. Attack arcs are also thicker and brighter in this mode so they are easier to follow. The **Both** and **Device traffic** layers are unchanged.
