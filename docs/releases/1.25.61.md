# LanPulse 1.25.61

## Changed
- **Threats map, Attacks layer:** with only **Attacks** selected, the map now keeps showing attack arcs even when the network is quiet. It replays your most recent attacks, one new arc every second and a half with up to three flying at once, and real new attacks still appear the moment they arrive. Attack arcs are also thicker and brighter in this mode so they are easier to follow. The **Both** and **Device traffic** layers keep their normal line weight.
- **Threats map colours:** attack arcs are now red (research scanners stay grey) and device-traffic arcs are blue, so the two are easy to tell apart when both are on. An arc to a known attacker or botnet server stays red.
