# LanPulse 1.39.12

## Added
- **Control over what each alert channel receives** (Settings > Channels). Every channel (ntfy, Telegram, Discord/webhook, email, IRC) now has:
  - **Send this channel:** all alerts, important and critical only, or critical only. Critical is a compromised device or a malware-domain lookup. Important adds exploited flaws, security warnings, honeypot and local-threat hits, and services going down. Everything else (new devices, attack bursts, unusual traffic) counts as routine.
  - **Hourly limit:** 5, 10, 20 or 50 per hour. When the limit is reached one notice says so, the rest are held, and the next message after the hour says how many were held back.
- **Repeat the same alert** (Settings > Alerts): wait 10 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours or 24 hours before the same alert about the same device is sent again.

The daily summary and the test button ignore these limits. Existing settings keep working: every channel starts on "All alerts" with no limit.
