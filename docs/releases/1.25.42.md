# LanPulse 1.25.42

## See who a device talks to
The device panel's **Where it connects** now lists the places the device connected to over the last 24 hours, one row per owner: the country, who owns it, the service (HTTPS, DNS, NTP, MQTT and so on), how many connections, how many were blocked, and when it last connected. Several addresses owned by one company are a single row. Attackers and known botnet servers are marked in red and listed first, and the usual warning about them stays at the top. Five rows show by default, and **Show all** lists the rest. This uses your router's firewall log, so it needs the outbound log turned on, as the old owner bars did.

## A shorter device panel
Timeline, Identification, Network, System, Open ports and Customize now fold, so a device opens short. A folded row still shows its main fact, such as the IP address and vendor or the number of open ports. Security findings, vulnerabilities, DNS and local-threat alerts, uptime and the connections stay open. LanPulse remembers which sections you opened.

## A calmer map when zoomed out
Zoom the topology out and it now shows names, icons and cables only, instead of a wall of tiny text. The addresses, speeds and link labels fade back in as you zoom in. Exporting the map still includes everything.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates > Download > Install. Your devices, history, settings and login are kept.
