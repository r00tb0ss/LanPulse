# LanPulse 1.39.31

## Public scan form is readable and no longer zooms the page
- The target box and scanner menu on the Threats > Public scan tab had a white background with pale text, so you could not read what you typed. They now match the dashboard theme.
- Phone browsers zoom the whole page when you tap a box with small text, which pushed the card off the right edge. The boxes now use 16px text so the page stays put, and the cards cannot grow wider than the screen.
- The browser check now fails if these boxes are unreadable, too small, or the page becomes wider than the screen.

## Fix: the map came back blank after visiting Public scan
- Switching from the Public scan section back to Attacks left the attack map empty. The map is now rebuilt the same way it is when you open the Threats tab, and the browser check fails if it comes back blank.

- Public scan history now lists every OpenVAS finding (it was capped at 8 while the header showed the full count), and long CVE lists wrap instead of running off the screen.
