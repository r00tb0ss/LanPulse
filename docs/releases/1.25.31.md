# LanPulse 1.25.31

## Blocked IPs that are still connecting now say so
The green **blocked** badge only means an IP is in LanPulse's blocklist group on your UDM. The group does nothing until a UniFi firewall policy uses it. If a blocked IP keeps reaching your network while LanPulse is watching, the badge turns red ("blocked, still connecting"), the IP panel says the same, and a note appears above the Live feed pointing you to the UniFi setup.

## Clearer UniFi setup steps
The one-time policy instructions in Settings > UniFi now say to use destination zone **Internal** (port-forwarded traffic such as the honeypot's arrives there), action Block, protocol All, and explain what the badge does and doesn't mean.

The warning only fires for connections seen live after the page loads; it can't tell you about hits that happened before.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
