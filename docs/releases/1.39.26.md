# LanPulse 1.39.26

## Public IP scanning has its own home
- The authorized public-IP scan (Nmap or OpenVAS) moved from the attacker panel to its own card on the Vulns tab, with its scan history, progress and results.
- The Threats attacker panel is now about the attacker only: what it tried, where it came from, reputation, AI investigation and blocking.
- Nothing about the scan changed: it is still manual, needs the ownership/authorization tick and a confirmation, is rate limited, and never targets an attacker IP automatically.
