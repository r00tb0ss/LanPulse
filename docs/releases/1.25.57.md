
## Research scanners
Each new attacker address is looked up once to see whether it belongs to a known research scanner such as Shodan or Censys. If your DNS server was busy or unreachable at that moment, the answer was saved as "not a scanner" for good, so a harmless scanner could keep raising alerts. A failed lookup is now not saved, and LanPulse asks again after 10 minutes.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update, or `git pull origin main && sudo ./install.sh`.
