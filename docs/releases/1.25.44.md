# LanPulse 1.25.44

## Shared switch ports still show their traffic
Devices that share one switch port, such as virtual machines on one host, can't each be given the port's traffic, so the last version showed them as idle. Their cables now carry the flow for the whole port again, each device is labeled "shares port 2" instead of "idle", and its details show the port's total download and upload as **Port total**, along with how many devices share it. No device is shown the port's total as if it were its own.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates > Download > Install. Your devices, history, settings and login are kept.
