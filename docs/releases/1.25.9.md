# LanPulse 1.25.9

## Honeypot
- Hits on the built-in honeypot (and external honeypot sensors) from the internet now show up on the Threats map, in the live feed and in the attacker lists, marked "Honeypot".
- They don't raise "attack reached your device" alerts, since a decoy was hit, not a real service.
- Only new hits appear; past hits stay on the Honeypot tab only.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
