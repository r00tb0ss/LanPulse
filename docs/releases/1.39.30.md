# LanPulse 1.39.30

## Choose the AI model per question
- The attacker investigation, captured-sample analysis and analyst chat now show an **AI model** drop-down listing the models your Ollama (or OpenAI-compatible) server has installed. Pick a fast model such as an 8B for quick answers and keep a 32B for deeper ones. Your choice is remembered in the browser and is never saved to the AI settings.
- Settings > Analyst suggests the installed models as you type the default model.
- Only models the AI server actually reports are accepted; anything else falls back to the configured model.

## The AI analyst knows today's date
- Every AI request now carries today's date and tells the model that its training data is older than LanPulse's live data, so it no longer calls 2026 CVEs "future" or dismisses entries it does not recognize.
