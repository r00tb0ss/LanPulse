# LanPulse 1.25.34

## Honeypot: counts no longer stop at 2,000
The Honeypot page worked out its totals from only the newest 2,000 hits, so "Hits" sat at 2000 (and the unique sources, per-service counts, logins tried and activity chart were all understated) once a busy week passed that. The totals are now counted over the whole time range in the database. The recent list below still shows the newest entries. Nothing stored was lost; only the display was capped.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
