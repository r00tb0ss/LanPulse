
## Local threat alerts from background intake
When a router log or honeypot hit from a device on your own network arrived through a background task, the live update and the alert for it were skipped. Both are now sent.

## Fewer missed packets
The network watcher looked up devices while the device list was changing and could throw away a packet when that happened. It now works from a copy.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update, or `git pull origin main && sudo ./install.sh`.
