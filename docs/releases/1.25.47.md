
## Idle devices on a shared port say "idle"
Virtual machines that share a switch port with their host showed "shares port 2" even when UniFi was reporting them at zero traffic. When UniFi gives a device its own reading, zero included, the map now shows "idle". Devices UniFi gives no reading for still say "shares port N", and a shared port's total is still never shown as one device's own.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update, or `git pull origin main && sudo ./install.sh`.
