# LanPulse 1.25.5

## Topology
- Fixed: clicking a device still opened the full device page before the small inspector. It now opens only the small inspector.
- Traffic is now clearly visible: every device gets a bar for its connections in the last hour, switches and access points show their total, links thicken with the traffic through them, and the inspector shows the count.
- Traffic covers all devices (it was limited to the 12 busiest). If no connection logs are coming in yet, the top bar says so and where to set it up.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates. No `install.sh --upgrade` needed.
