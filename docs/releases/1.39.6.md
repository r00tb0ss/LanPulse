# 1.39.6

Live map: traffic arcs no longer shoot up from below the bottom of the map.

- The blue device-traffic arcs bulged south, so a long route (for example your network to Australia) swung out past the bottom edge and came back up. Attack arcs did the same when the attacker was east of you. Every arc now bulges north, like a flight path, and very long routes bulge less.
- The browser check in CI now tests that a long route to the southern hemisphere stays on the map.

No change to any detection, score or data.
