# LanPulse 1.25.39

## Hide the left menu
The menu button in the top bar now works on a computer too: click it to hide the left menu, so the map and the Rings, Tiles and Table views get the full width, and click it again to bring the menu back. LanPulse remembers your choice. On a phone the menu still slides over the map as before.

## Traffic flow like the UniFi topology
With **Traffic** turned on, the topology now shows download and upload separately. Blue dots travel toward a device for download and purple dots travel toward the gateway for upload, each at the speed of its own direction, and busy links get a soft glow. Rates such as `↓ 178 Kbps  ↑ 13.9 Kbps` appear beside each device, each switch and access point link, and the internet link, and in a device's details as **Down / up**. This needs UniFi connected; a link that UniFi gives no direction for keeps the single pulse.

UniFi reports upload and download from the access point's side, so if they look swapped next to the UniFi app, click **Swap ↑↓** in the toolbar. LanPulse remembers that too.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates > Download > Install. Your devices, history, settings and login are kept.
