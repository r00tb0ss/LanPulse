# LanPulse 1.25.46

## Wired devices and virtual machines show their own speed
UniFi reports a Wi-Fi device's speed under one set of names and a wired device's under another, and LanPulse was only reading the first. Every wired device therefore looked as if UniFi had no speed for it, and devices sharing a switch port, such as virtual machines on one host, showed nothing of their own. LanPulse now reads the wired names too, so each of those devices shows its own download and upload, as the UniFi app does. A shared port's total is still shown separately, as **Port total**, and is never given to one device as its own.

## Upgrade
Settings > Update > Check for updates > Download > Install. Your devices, history, settings and login are kept.
